Soil NPK & Micronutrient Diagnostic Matrix

Optical and electrochemical soil probes constantly evaluate nutrient bioavailability. Use this field diagnostic guide to identify plant symptoms and execute immediate corrective bio-remediation:

Nutrient Element Visual Field Symptom Impact on TN Crops Sensor Warning Level Scientific Remediation & Bio-Prescription
Nitrogen (N) General chlorosis: bottom older leaves turn uniform pale yellowish green, spreading upward; stunted tillering. Drastic drop in paddy tillers and tea shoot flushing. < 200 kg/ha available N Foliar spray of 2% Urea (20g/L water) or Azospirillum slurry root dip. Incorporate green manure (Daincha / Sunnhemp).
Phosphorus (P) Leaves turn dark bronze-green with reddish-purple pigmentation along leaf veins; delayed flowering. Poor root anchorage in Cauvery Delta clay soils; reduced grain panicle count. < 20 kg/ha available P Apply Single Super Phosphate (SSP) or Rock Phosphate enriched with Phosphobacteria (2 kg/acre mixed in FYM).
Potassium (K) Marginal leaf scorch: leaf edges and tips turn rusty brown and curl downward (firing symptom). Weak stems leading to crop lodging; poor copra kernel weight in Pollachi coconut palms. < 150 kg/ha available K Soil application of Muriate of Potash (MOP) 25-35 kg/acre. Foliar spray of Potassium Schoenite (1%) at flowering.
Zinc (Zn) "Khaira" disease: rusty brown blotches on upper third of paddy leaf; mid-rib bleaching. Common in waterlogged alluvial paddy soils where zinc precipitates as insoluble hydroxide. < 0.8 ppm DTPA-Zn Soil application of Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO₄) 25 kg/ha. Do not mix directly with DAP to avoid chemical lockout.
Iron (Fe) Interveinal chlorosis: young emerging top leaves turn ivory white while veins remain faint green. Frequent in Madurai / Tirunelveli alkaline calcareous soils (pH > 7.8). < 4.0 ppm DTPA-Fe Foliar spray of Ferrous Sulphate 1% + Citric Acid 0.1% at 10-day intervals until greening resumes.
Boron (B) Button shedding in coconut palms; hollow core in cauliflower; crinkling of young tea flush tips. Severe yield loss in Pollachi palms (>40% button drop). < 0.5 ppm hot water B Apply 50g Borax (Disodium Octaborate) per coconut palm in circular ring basin twice annually with compost.
PROTOCOL 01 — FIELD CAPACITY

Soil Moisture Tensiometer Rules

Smart solenoid valves trigger automatically based on soil texture and Available Water Capacity (AWC):

  • Sandy Loam (Erode / Western Zone): Actuates when soil moisture drops below 55%; stops at 82%.
  • Clay Alluvium (Delta Zone): Maintains 2-4 cm thin saturation layer; avoids deep water ponding.
  • Acidic Laterite (The Nilgiris): Actuation suppressed during morning mist and relative humidity >85%.
PROTOCOL 02 — EVAPOTRANSPIRATION

Weather-Adaptive Water Budgeting

The IoT gateway continuously computes daily Crop Evapotranspiration (ETc = ETo × Kc):

  • Coconut Palms: 40–55 Liters per palm per day during summer; 20–25 Liters during cooler months.
  • GI Turmeric: 6,000 Liters/acre/day delivered in 2 split pulses (morning 06:00 and evening 17:30).
  • Rainfall Forecast Interlock: If radar detects >10 mm rainfall incoming within 12 hours, scheduled cycles hold automatically.
PROTOCOL 03 — PRESSURE & EMITTERS

Drip Line Maintenance & Anti-Clogging

Tamil Nadu groundwater often has high dissolved carbonates. Prevent emitter clogging with routine flushes:

  • Operate inline sand media and disc filters at 2.0 to 2.5 kg/cm² operating pressure.
  • Execute quarterly acid flushing with 0.1% Phosphoric / Hydrochloric acid to clear salt encrustations.
  • Flush sub-main flush valves every fortnight for 3 minutes until clear water discharges.
PROTOCOL 04 — ENERGY MANAGEMENT

TNEB Agri Feeder & Solar Hybrid Sync

Platform integrates with Tamil Nadu's 3-phase agricultural power timing:

  • Automated synchronization with TANGEDCO 3-phase supply slots (Day / Night rotational cycles).
  • Solar DC pump variable frequency drives (VFD) ramp water delivery during peak solar radiation (11:00 to 14:00).
  • SMS alert dispatched to farmer's mobile phone if motor trips due to phase reversal or dry run.
